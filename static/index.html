About the Project & Data Lineage

Solving agricultural planning gaps for Nigerian farmers through open-access climate telemetry.

Nigerian Agriculture Context

Nigerian smallholder farmers frequently experience crop losses and poor financial planning due to uncertain yield expectations. By combining state-level daily satellite weather data from the NASA POWER API with FAOSTAT ground-truth statistics, this MVP provides accessible data-driven yield forecasts and actionable agronomic recommendations.

Data Sources & Provenance

  • NASA POWER Agroclimatology API: Daily rainfall, air temperature, relative humidity, and solar radiation for 37 Nigerian states (2015–2024).
  • FAOSTAT: UN Food and Agriculture Organization national crop yield ($kg/ha$) and area harvested data for Nigeria.
  • Kaggle Agronomic Data: Representative soil nutrient requirement profiles ($N, P, K, pH$) across major staple crops.
